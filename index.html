Cargo Cove: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026. Publisher: Imran Ali (silent.lips125@gmail.com).

Cargo Cove is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses and the choices you have.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings and purchases) is stored on your device. The save file is signed so that changes made outside the game can be detected. We do not ask you to create an account.

Information collected by our partners

When you play with ads or analytics enabled, our partners may collect:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and approximate location derived from your IP address, to deliver and measure ads.
  • Game activity (levels played, ads watched, purchases made), to understand how the game is used and to fix problems.
  • Crash and performance data, to keep the game stable.

Partners: Google AdMob (ads and consent), Google Firebase (analytics, crash reports, anonymous sign-in, the daily board, remote settings and App Check) and Google Play (purchases and integrity checks).

Online features: the daily board

The daily challenge has an optional online board. To use it the game signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication, which creates a random user ID (not linked to your name, email or phone). When you finish the daily challenge the game sends your user ID, a display name (a generated name such as "Captain 4F2A"), your move count and the level number to Google Cloud Firestore, so that others can see the board. The game also uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity, which sends a signal from Google Play to confirm that the app and device are genuine. If the device or the saved game looks modified, the online board is switched off for it. The game works fully offline without these features.

Reminders

If you turn on Reminders in Settings, the game schedules notifications on your device (no server is involved) and asks Android for permission to show them. They are cancelled when you open the game and you can turn them off at any time.

Ads and your choices

The first time you open the game you can choose personalised ads or limited ads (not based on your interests). You can change this at any time under Settings > Ad choices. On iOS you can also refuse tracking in the system prompt, and on both platforms you can reset your advertising ID in the device settings. You can remove full-screen ads with the Remove ads purchase; optional reward ads stay available because you choose to watch them.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. Restore purchases is in Settings.

Children

Cargo Cove is meant for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to certain processing. Because we do not run accounts, most data is held by our partners or on your device; contact us at silent.lips125@gmail.com and we will help. Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device.

Deleting your data

The game has no accounts. The only data we hold about you is the anonymous daily board entry (user ID, display name, move count). To have it deleted, email us as described below and send the user ID shown under Settings once available, or the display name and date; we will remove it. Everything else is on your device. To delete the data held on your device, clear the game's storage in your device settings or uninstall the game. To ask for data collected by our partners (advertising and analytics) to be deleted, email silent.lips125@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Cargo Cove data deletion" and, if you can, your advertising ID (Settings > Google > Ads on Android). We will pass the request on and reply within 30 days. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID yourself in your device settings.

Retention and security

Partner data is kept as long as each partner's own policy says. Connections use HTTPS.

Changes

We will update this page when the game changes and show the new date above.
