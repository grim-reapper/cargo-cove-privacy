Cargo Cove: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026. Publisher: Imran Ali (silent.lips125@gmail.com).

Cargo Cove is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses and the choices you have.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings and purchases) is stored on your device only. We do not create accounts.

Information collected by our partners

When you play with ads or analytics enabled, our partners may collect:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and approximate location derived from your IP address, to deliver and measure ads.
  • Game activity (levels played, ads watched, purchases made), to understand how the game is used and to fix problems.
  • Crash and performance data, to keep the game stable.

Partners: Google AdMob (ads and consent), Google Firebase (analytics, crash reports), the Apple App Store and Google Play (purchases).

Ads and your choices

The first time you open the game you can choose personalised ads or limited ads (not based on your interests). You can change this at any time under Settings > Ad choices. On iOS you can also refuse tracking in the system prompt, and on both platforms you can reset your advertising ID in the device settings. You can remove full-screen ads with the Remove ads purchase; optional reward ads stay available because you choose to watch them.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. Restore purchases is in Settings.

Children

Cargo Cove is meant for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to certain processing. Because we do not run accounts, most data is held by our partners or on your device; contact us at silent.lips125@gmail.com and we will help. Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device.

Deleting your data

The game has no accounts, so there is nothing to delete on our side beyond what is on your device. To delete the data held on your device, clear the game's storage in your device settings or uninstall the game. To ask for data collected by our partners (advertising and analytics) to be deleted, email silent.lips125@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Cargo Cove data deletion" and, if you can, your advertising ID (Settings > Google > Ads on Android). We will pass the request on and reply within 30 days. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID yourself in your device settings.

Retention and security

Partner data is kept as long as each partner's own policy says. Connections use HTTPS.

Changes

We will update this page when the game changes and show the new date above.
